# NewzGator Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 12 October 2026

NewzGator is a news reader for Android that brings together stories from Bangladeshi and English-language publishers. It is developed and operated by the NewzGator team ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version: you can read everything without an account; the app sends a random device identifier and your reading actions to our server so that saved stories, reactions and recommendations work; signing in is optional and adds only the name, email and photo your sign-in provider shares; we show no ads and sell no data.

## 1. What we collect

### Without an account

- **A device identifier.** On first launch the app generates a random identifier (for example `usr_3f9c…`) that is stored on your phone and sent with every request to our server. It is not linked to your name, phone number or any other account unless you sign in. It lets saved stories, reactions and comments belong to your installation.
- **Reading actions.** When you open a story, react to it (like, dislike, important, inaccurate), save it, or post a comment, the app sends that action to our server together with the device identifier. Opened stories are used to rank the "For you" recommendations.
- **Technical data.** Our server records the IP address and the app's user-agent string with each request, as every web server does. We use them for rate limiting and to diagnose failures. Server logs are kept for a short period.
- **A push notification token.** If you allow notifications, Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging issues a token to your device so that breaking news and daily briefings can be delivered. The token is held by Firebase; our server sends messages to a topic that every installation subscribes to and does not keep a list of tokens.

### If you sign in

Signing in with Google, Facebook or X is optional. Everything in the app works without it; signing in keeps your saved stories, reactions and comments when you change or reset your phone. When you sign in, your provider shares your **name, email address and profile picture**, which we store with your account. We never receive your password.

### Stored only on your phone

Your reading history, reading position in long stories, offline copies of stories you opened, reading statistics (stories this week, finished, day streak), the stories cached for the home screen widget, and your settings (language, theme, text size, voice) stay on your device. They are not sent to us. You can clear them at any time from Settings, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.

## 2. What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or any advertising identifier. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software.

## 3. How we use the data

- To show you the news, your saved stories and your comments and reactions.
- To rank the "For you" rail from the stories you have opened and saved.
- To send breaking news and daily briefing notifications, when you allow notifications.
- To keep the service reliable and secure, including rate limiting.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Who else receives data

- **Publishers.** Story photos and publisher logos are loaded directly from the publishers' own websites, so those sites see your IP address when images load, as they would in a web browser. Opening "Read the original" opens the publisher's site in your browser, where the publisher's own privacy policy applies.
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** delivers push notifications and holds your notification token. Google's privacy policy applies to that service.
- **Sign-in providers** (Google, Facebook, X) when you choose to sign in with them.
- **Hosting.** Our server and database run on Amazon Web Services. Anonymous service metrics (request counts and timings, server health) are sent to Grafana Cloud for monitoring; they contain no personal data.
- **Public content.** Comments you post are visible to every reader of that story, together with the name and profile picture from your account.

We do not share data with anyone else, except if required by law.

## 5. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS. Sign-in session data on your phone is stored in encrypted storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Retention

Account data and your reading actions are kept while your account or installation is active. Server logs containing IP addresses are kept for no more than 30 days. Notification history is kept to show the in-app notification list.

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **Notifications:** turn them off in your phone's app settings at any time.
- **Reading history, offline copies, searches, statistics:** clear them from Settings in the app.
- **Sign out:** from the Profile screen. Your account data stays on our server until you delete the account.
- **Delete your account and data:** see [Deleting your account](delete-account). Deletion removes your account, saved stories, reactions and comments from our server. Reading actions recorded under the device identifier alone are deleted on request with that identifier.
- **Access or correction:** email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

## 8. Children

NewzGator is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will also be announced in the app.

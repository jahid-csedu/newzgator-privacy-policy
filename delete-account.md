# Deleting your NewzGator account and data

You can have your NewzGator account and the data linked to it deleted at any time.

## How to request deletion

Email **jahid.csedu@gmail.com** from the address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my NewzGator account". We confirm the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.

If you never signed in and want the reading actions recorded under your installation's device identifier removed, send the same email with the identifier shown under **About** in the app.

An in-app "Delete account" option on the Profile screen is being added; once it is available, deletion from the app is immediate.

## What is deleted

- Your account record (name, email address, profile picture) and the link to your sign-in provider.
- Saved stories, reactions and comments made from the account.
- Reading actions used for recommendations.

Data stored only on your phone (reading history, offline copies, settings) is removed when you uninstall the app, or earlier from Settings.

## What is not deleted

- Anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example how many readers opened a story) that cannot identify you.
- Server logs containing IP addresses, which expire on their own within 30 days.

# Contact us

[Privacy Policy](./) · [Deleting your account](delete-account) · Contact us

**NewzGator** is a news reader for Android that brings together stories from Bangladeshi and English-language publishers. Every story shows its publisher and links to the original article. The app is developed and operated by **CodeGrid**, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

## How to reach us

- **Email:** [newzgator2025@gmail.com](mailto:newzgator2025@gmail.com)
- **In the app:** open the menu (⋮) on any screen, choose *About NewzGator*, then *Contact us*.
- **Location:** Dhaka, Bangladesh

We answer within 30 days, usually much sooner. Write to us about:

- a story that is wrong, missing, or should not be in the app
- a publisher who wants their content added or removed
- questions about your data, or a request to delete it (see [Deleting your account](delete-account))
- bugs, feature ideas, or anything else

For questions about your data, see the [Privacy Policy](./).
